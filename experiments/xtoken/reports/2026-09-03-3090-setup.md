No downgrade/relock was performed. Single-GPU CUDA execution does not validate
NCCL multi-GPU behavior. The historical install action failed at its final
`uv pip check`, after installation and the CUDA probe succeeded.

Synthetic P-KL shapes were `[1,128,128256]` student / `[1,160,151936]` teacher,
and `[1,256,128256]` / `[1,288,151936]`. Peaks were approximately 0.561 / 1.101
GiB **for loss/logits/optimizer only**, excluding language models. These cannot
be used as model-training throughput, memory, cost or scaling estimates.

P-KL values were about -0.25 to -0.26. The tested upstream helper averages teacher
log-probabilities by chunk without renormalizing the teacher distribution after
that averaging. For these random inputs, passing finite execution/autograd is
not evidence of a normalized, nonnegative KL or model-quality improvement. The
harness did not modify this upstream formula.

Missing gates: actual tokenizer/projection, model weights, Automodel worker
initialization, real corpus, trainer/optimizer updates on model parameters,
checkpoint, held-out evaluation and distributed execution. The synthetic smoke
does not justify upgrading to two GPUs by itself.
